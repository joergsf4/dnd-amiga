D&D Dungeon Crawler (Amiga) in the browser

A fan project inspired by Baldur's Gate 3 and Dungeons & Dragons; not affiliated with Larian
Studios or Wizards of the Coast. All graphics, music and sounds are made for this project.

The disk image (dnd.adf) boots like a real Amiga 500 floppy. In the browser it runs on
vAmigaWeb (GPL-3.0, https://github.com/vAmigaWeb/vAmigaWeb), a WebAssembly build of Dirk
Hoffmann's vAmiga, hosted unmodified at https://vAmigaWeb.github.io/. Because a real Kickstart
ROM is copyrighted and cannot be published, the emulator uses AROS (https://aros.sourceforge.io/)
instead -- a free, open-source reimplementation of AmigaOS -- which vAmigaWeb bundles for exactly
this purpose.
